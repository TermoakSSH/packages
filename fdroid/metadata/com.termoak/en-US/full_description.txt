Termoak is a modern SSH client to manage your servers from your phone or tablet, with the same sessions, hosts and keys as on your computer.

SESSIONS THAT NEVER DROP
Open the terminal on the Termoak server and pick it up where you left it: switch devices, lose coverage or lock your phone, and when you come back everything is still there, with its history and its processes.

A COMPLETE TERMINAL
• SSH with passwords, keys (Ed25519, RSA, ECDSA) and reusable identities
• Jump hosts, proxy, agent forwarding and keep-alive
• Extra keys bar (Esc, Ctrl, Tab, arrows…) and gestures
• Snippets: save commands and run them on one or many servers at once
• Port forwarding (tunnels)
• Confirmation before pasting several lines
• Automatic detection of each host's system

TABLETS AND FOLDABLES
Split view with up to four terminals and broadcast typing to all of them.

ENCRYPTED, SYNCED VAULT
Hosts, keys, identities and snippets encrypted and synced across all your devices. Secrets never travel in plain text. Organize them in vaults: a personal one, others shared with people or your team, with Editor or Use-only permissions (connect without seeing passwords).

SHARE YOUR TERMINAL
Invite a person, your team or create a link. By default they can only watch; they can ask for the keyboard and you decide when to give it and for how long.

BACKGROUND AI
Ask the AI to investigate a failure or prepare a deployment. It works on its own and asks for your approval before running anything sensitive. Works with your own API keys.

SEVERAL ACCOUNTS
Use the official server termoak.com or your own Termoak server, even several at once.

SECURITY
Two-step verification, encrypted secrets and open source (AGPL-3.0), so you can audit it or run your own server.

Free, no ads, no card required. Also available for Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS.
